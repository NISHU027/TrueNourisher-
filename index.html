OUR STANDARD / A PROPOSED JOURNEY

From plant to pack,
with proof at every step.

A professional making process starts with records and testing, not just a beautiful label. These are the standards trueNourisher intends to build toward; they do not describe a verified supplier or current manufacturing operation.

01

Know the source

Record the supplier, origin, botanical name, plant part, harvest or lot details, and chain of custody for each ingredient.

02

Check every lot

Set acceptance specifications and verify identity, purity, and relevant contaminants with documented, fit-for-purpose testing.

03

Define the formula

Disclose ingredients, amounts per serving, plant parts, extract ratios, excipients, and evidence that applies to the actual formulation.

04

Make under controls

Choose a qualified, appropriately licensed manufacturer; document the process, hygiene controls, batch records, and packaging checks.

05

Release with records

Review batch results against specifications, retain traceability records, and publish a batch summary only when verified.

Proposed brand standard only. No manufacturing partner, certification, laboratory result, product formula, or batch record is represented as established. Applicable requirements depend on the final product category and should be confirmed before launch. See FSSAI’s health supplement and nutraceutical standards ↗

TEXTUAL ROOTS / READ THE SOURCES

Honour the tradition.
Be precise about what it says.

The Vedas offer poetic and cultural reflections on plants and the natural world. They are not modern product specifications, clinical trials, or manufacturing instructions. We use these references as historical context and keep product evidence separate.

Ṛgveda · 10.97

Hymn to the plants

A hymn addressed to plants, expressing reverence for their presence and qualities. This is a textual reference, not evidence for a modern supplement claim.
